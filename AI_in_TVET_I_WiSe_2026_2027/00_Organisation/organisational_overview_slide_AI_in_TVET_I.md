## Start: Introduction to AI & Prompting

Every seminar starts with an introduction to Artificial Intelligence. **Part I is live today, Part II is a self-learning module.**

{{1}}
<section>

| Building block       | Content                                                                                       |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **How AI works**     | Machine learning vs. generative AI; Large Language Models: tokens, probabilities, training data, context window; multimodal models |
| **Prompting**        | Role · Task · Context · Format · Examples; iterative refinement; prompt patterns and the CLEAR framework |
| **Tool usage**       | Discussion (ChatGPT, Claude) · Research (Perplexity, Consensus) · Content creation for LiaScript · agents and workflows |
| **Limitations**      | Hallucinations, fabricated references, outdated knowledge, bias, missing local and cultural context |
| **Ethics**           | Data protection, copyright, transparency, human agency and accountability, sustainability (UNESCO human-centred mindset) |

</section>

{{2}}
<section>

**Rules for this seminar**

- Document every AI use in your **AI tools log**: tool, purpose, prompt, output, your evaluation.
- Verify facts and references against primary sources.
- Never enter personal data of learners or colleagues into AI tools.

</section>

## Learning Objectives

Aligned with the UNESCO 2025 AI Competency Frameworks (Students & Teachers), this seminar aims to:

{{1}}
<section>

1. Introduce **trends in TVET and the labour market** shaped by AI and digitalisation.
2. Build basic **AI literacy**: definitions, system components and key applications.
3. Foster critical reflection on **AI ethics**: fairness, transparency, inclusion, sustainability and accountability.
4. Establish **human-centred principles**: human agency, accountability and social responsibility.
5. Explore the role of **AI in pedagogy** and in vocational learning scenarios.
6. Introduce **OER principles** and the potential of AI for open learning resources and micro-credentials.

</section>
