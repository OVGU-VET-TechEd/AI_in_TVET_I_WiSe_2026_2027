About the Seminar

This seminar introduces the foundations and applications of Artificial Intelligence in Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET).

What we cover

  • Global frameworks: UNESCO AI Competency Frameworks for Students and Teachers, UNESCO ICT-CFT, EU AI Act, UNESCO TVET Strategy
  • Risks and opportunities of AI for learners, teachers and institutions
  • Current developments: generative AI tools, AI workflows, educational agents
  • Labour market trends: Industry 4.0/5.0, automation, green skills, lifelong learning
  • Open Educational Resources (OER), licensing and micro-credentials

Compact format

6 sessions in 6 weeks – half of them self-learning sessions, so please plan your time carefully. AI in TVET II follows from 03.12.2026 (Thursdays, 09:00–12:00).

Learning Objectives

Learning Objectives

  1. Introduce trends in TVET and the labour market shaped by AI and digitalisation.
  2. Build basic AI literacy: definitions, system components and key applications.
  3. Foster critical reflection on AI ethics: fairness, transparency, inclusion, sustainability and accountability.
  4. Establish human-centred principles: human agency, accountability and social responsibility.
  5. Explore the role of AI in pedagogy and in vocational learning scenarios.
  6. Introduce OER principles and the potential of AI for open learning resources and micro-credentials.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of the seminar, you will be able to …

  • explain how generative AI works and write effective, iterative prompts for TVET tasks,
  • analyse AI-driven trends in a TVET sector using UNESCO and EU frameworks,
  • evaluate AI tools for their pedagogical value, limitations and ethical risks,
  • design a TVET-focused micro-credential (EU approach) with AI-supported LiaScript learning nuggets,
  • pitch your concept in a LiaScript presentation and reflect critically on your AI use with the UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers.

Artificial Intelligence in this Seminar

Every seminar starts with an introduction to Artificial Intelligence. Part I is live (15.10.), Part II is a self-learning module (22.10.).

Building blockContent
How AI worksMachine learning vs. generative AI; Large Language Models: tokens, probabilities, training data, context window; multimodal models
PromptingRole · Task · Context · Format · Examples; iterative refinement; prompt patterns and the CLEAR framework
Tool usageDiscussion (ChatGPT, Claude) · Research (Perplexity, Consensus) · Content creation for LiaScript · agents and workflows
LimitationsHallucinations, fabricated references, outdated knowledge, bias, missing local and cultural context
EthicsData protection, copyright, transparency, human agency and accountability, sustainability (UNESCO human-centred mindset)
Rules for this seminar
  • Document every AI use in your AI tools log: tool, purpose, prompt, output, your evaluation.
  • Verify facts and references against primary sources.
  • Never enter personal data of learners or colleagues into AI tools.
